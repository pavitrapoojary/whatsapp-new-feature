The booking is shared in WhatsApp.
The payment should start there too.

Why Splitwise would have to change

Splitwise keeps the ledger. It does not own the chat or the payment

🚧

A daily cap

3–4 free entries a day. A trip or a month-end hits the wall fast.
💳

A price

₹149 a month or ₹999 a year in India, for what a chat can do for free.
📲

Everyone installs

The group only works if every friend joins the app.
↗️

Pay somewhere else

It tracks who owes. The actual UPI payment happens in another app.

My view

  • Splitting is becoming a feature of chat and payments, not a product on its own.
  • Splitwise would have to add UPI payments and live inside chats, or lose the everyday small splits.
  • It keeps its strength in long trips, many currencies, complex shares.

Fair counterpoint

  • Splitwise also shows up in the Share sheet and has years of history in people's groups.
  • WhatsApp Pay is still a small player in UPI. It must earn trust on basics first.
  • So this is an opinion to test, not a certainty.
How it works

Same Share button. WhatsApp reads what comes with it

1
You book in any app and pay the shop the usual way.
2
You tap Share and choose WhatsApp, as always.
3
WhatsApp notices a booking or bill and a private card appears for you only.
4
You pick Split now, Add to monthly digest, or Not now.
5
Friends tap Pay inside the chat. Paid ✓ shows up live.

Way 1 · Day one

WhatsApp reads what you just shared (screenshot, PDF or text) on your phone and asks you to confirm. Merchants do nothing.

Way 2 · Later

Merchants send the booking details along with the same Share. More accurate, no screenshot reading. Still no new button.

Honest note: today a Share carries text, links and files, so Way 1 comes first. Way 2 needs merchants to join once the habit is proven.

Trust and risks

It only works if people feel safe

Rules built in

🔒
The nudge is visible only to you.
📤
Reads only what you choose to share. It never scans your gallery.
📱
Reading happens on your phone. Chats stay end-to-end encrypted.
✏️
You confirm and edit the amount and people before anything is sent.
🔕
"Don't suggest in this chat" is always one tap away.

Biggest risks, and the answer

1
"WhatsApp is reading my chats."
Opt-in at the moment of Share, on-device, per-chat off switch.
2
Payments failing break trust.
Early WhatsApp Pay users reported failed payments and switched apps. Reliability first.
3
Wrong amount detected.
Always a confirm step.
4
A friend has no WhatsApp Pay.
Request opens in any UPI app.
Failed-payment point: Outlook Business.

Conclusion
WhatsApp does not have to win the checkout. It can win what happens right after it.

What I learned

1
The real problem is the gap between where we talk and where we pay.
2
The fix needs no new button. The Share we already use is enough.
3
Splitwise-style apps are exposed: a cap, a price, an install, and payment elsewhere.
4
Everyone gains: payer, friends, merchants, WhatsApp, regulator.

If I were the PM

Build first

The private nudge after a shared booking.

Measure first

Nudge acceptance and time to full payment.

Protect always

Privacy, reliable payments, and the payer's dignity.

Biggest bet

Chat is where small group money should live.
